NASA Posts the Moon's Most Competitive Help-Wanted Ad

What happened

NASA opened applications on November 7 for its next astronaut-candidate class, with completed materials due by 11:59 p.m. EST on December 3. The agency expects to announce the final selection in late 2027. NASA says the candidates will train for lunar-surface operations and missions beyond Earth as the United States builds a Moon Base and develops systems for eventual crewed missions to Mars.

Why it matters

The announcement turns a moon program into a real, tightly specified federal hiring process. NASA lists U.S. citizenship, a qualifying advanced STEM or medical/test-pilot credential, at least three years of related professional experience, a long-duration flight physical and strong leadership, teamwork and communication skills. More than 8,000 people applied in 2024 and NASA selected 10 in 2025; the current corps has 32 active astronauts. An application is an entry to a multi-year selection and training pipeline, not a ticket to a particular flight.
