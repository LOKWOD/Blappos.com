A Used Mario Box Sells for $96,875 Because the Manual Survived

What happened

Heritage Auctions announced on November 25 that a PSA 9.0 complete-in-box copy of the first-production 1985 Super Mario Bros. sold for $96,875 in its November 20–21 video-game auction. Heritage says the matte-sticker copy set records both for a first-production copy of the game and for any complete-in-box video game. Complete-in-box does not mean factory sealed: this lot included the cartridge, box, manual and original components after the package had been opened.

Why it matters

The price shows how the collectible-game market separates an intact early package from a loose cartridge or sealed copy. The record category, grade, production variant and surviving paper components all matter; dropping any of those qualifiers would turn a specific auction result into a misleading claim about every old copy. The $96,875 figure is the reported sale price for one graded lot, not an appraisal for ordinary household copies.
