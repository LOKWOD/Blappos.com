A Heart Surgeon Brings 3,200 Hearts Home From Work

What happened

Guinness World Records reported on November 10 that Swiss heart surgeon Diana Reser reclaimed the title for the largest collection of heart-shaped items with 3,200 objects. She first held the record in 2010 with 775, lost it in 2024 to a 1,560-item collection and completed the new official count in April. Her collection spans her home and office and includes clothing, ornaments, perfume, soap, a heart-shaped Rubik's cube, a working stethoscope, a Berlin Wall fragment and six heart tattoos.

Why it matters

The record is a classification and verification exercise as much as an accumulation contest: witnesses certified that the collection exists and the category requires heart-shaped items, not anatomical hearts or an estimate of gifts received. Reser says roughly half came from other people after colleagues learned about her chosen specialty. Guinness reports the certified count and title; it does not turn the collection into medical advice, a museum catalogue or a valuation of every object.
