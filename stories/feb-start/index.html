Nuclear limits expire from lack of interest

What happened

New START, the last nuclear-arms treaty limiting U.S. and Russian strategic arsenals, expired without a replacement.

Why it matters

For the first time in more than half a century, the world’s two largest nuclear arsenals were left without treaty caps.
