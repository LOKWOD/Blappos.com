THE STORY BEHIND THE SATIRE · A rescue lab · Aug 28

Cyborg cockroach applies for first responder

What happened

Australian researchers demonstrated electronically guided giant cockroaches that can carry sensors—and potentially small medical payloads—through confined disaster rubble.

Why it matters

Living insects can squeeze through voids that conventional rescue robots cannot, giving responders a possible way to locate and assist trapped survivors before larger equipment can reach them.

The Blappos angle
