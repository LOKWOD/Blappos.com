Poland Adds Full-Body Fire to the Zip-Line Waiver

What happened

UPI reported on November 20 that Polish professional stuntman Stanisław Madej traveled 568 feet 6.8 inches on a zip line while his full body was on fire, earning the Guinness World Records title for the farthest distance full-body burn on a zip wire. The attempt took place October 3 in Lodz to mark the tenth anniversary of the Tyrolka w Manufakturze zip line. Madej wore specialized protective clothing and water-based insulating gel, and a support team extinguished him after the run.

Why it matters

The publication clock and stunt clock are different: the certified result was reported November 20, but the attempt occurred October 3. The record is also evidence of a controlled professional performance, not evidence that fire became predictable or ordinary zip-line equipment became suitable for a burn stunt. Madej explicitly emphasized fire's danger, the need for awareness and concentration, and the protective preparation behind the spectacle.
