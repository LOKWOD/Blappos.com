Jet ski stunt on flooded Bangkok street ends with jail, fine and confiscation

What happened

A 27-year-old man rode a jet ski along flooded New Phetchaburi Road while vehicles and residents were navigating a wider flood emergency. Video showed the craft making sharp turns and producing large waves. Makkasan police detained the rider and charged him with causing a public nuisance and obstructing traffic; a court reduced a one-month jail sentence and 10,000-baht fine to 15 days and 5,000 baht after a guilty plea and cooperation, and ordered the jet ski confiscated. India Today reported the case on September 29.

Why it matters

The waves were not harmless stage effects: floodwater was already surrounding stalled vehicles, homes and public infrastructure during a disaster that had killed at least 23 people across Thailand by September 29. Authorities also charged the person recording the stunt. The broader lesson is behavioral and specific—turning an emergency route into content can add wake, risk and obstruction for people who did not consent to the show.
