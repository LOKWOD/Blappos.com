U.S. and China pledge a more stable relationship after Washington summit

What happened

Presidents Donald Trump and Xi Jinping met at the White House on September 24 during Xi's state visit. The Associated Press live report, citing the Chinese government readout, said they discussed the Middle East, Ukraine and the Korean Peninsula and decided to pursue a constructive, strategic and stable U.S.-China relationship. The visit unfolded while both governments were trying to preserve a fragile trade truce.

Why it matters

The United States and China shape global trade, technology, military risk and climate policy. A pledge to stabilize ties can lower the chance that disputes escalate by accident, but it is a diplomatic direction—not a detailed agreement or proof that the hardest conflicts have been resolved.
