What happened

The Senate passed a bill by voice vote to fund most of DHS while leaving ICE and part of Border Patrol funding for a separate fight.

Why it matters

The compromise offered an exit from a 42-day shutdown that had left workers unpaid and airport lines snarled, but House Republicans rejected the package later that day.

The Blappos angle
