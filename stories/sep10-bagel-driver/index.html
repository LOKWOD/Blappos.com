Driver discovers bagel preparation is not a hands-free feature

What happened

Queensland police said a 29-year-old provisional-license driver was caught steering a silver Honda HR-V with his knees while using both hands to butter a bagel in heavy traffic on Tor Street in Newtown, Toowoomba. Officers also found the vehicle was unregistered.

Why it matters

Police fined the driver A$1,104 and issued three demerit points. He reportedly admitted the choice was “stupid” and explained that he was hungry after working since morning; police used the incident to warn that even ordinary distractions can have serious consequences.

The Blappos angle

The driver kept both hands on breakfast and trusted the commute to his knees. Queensland has now added a useful road-safety rule: don’t schmear and steer.
