THE STORY BEHIND THE SATIRE · Washington, D.C. · Feb 14

Government closes one department at a time

What happened

Funding expired for the Department of Homeland Security after lawmakers failed to reach agreement over immigration-enforcement policy.

Why it matters

Essential personnel kept working while pay, airport operations and public services became bargaining chips.

The Blappos angle

The lights stayed on. The paychecks took a procedural recess.
