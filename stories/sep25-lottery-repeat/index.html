North Carolina woman wins the same $100,000 scratch-off prize twice

What happened

A Lexington, North Carolina, woman won a $100,000 top prize on the Ruby Red 7's scratch-off game, the North Carolina Education Lottery said. The September 25 report described it as her second $100,000 win on the same game, following another top-prize ticket in August 2025. After required tax withholding, the latest prize paid $72,016.

Why it matters

Winning once does not make a later ticket more likely to win; each ticket is governed by the game's published prize pool and odds. The repeat result is remarkable precisely because independent rare events can cluster without creating a system anyone can reliably copy. She said the first prize helped her four grandchildren and the new one would fund a beach trip with her husband.
