A Robot Becomes the Off-World Mileage King

What happened

Reuters reported on October 13 that NASA's six-wheeled Perseverance rover had driven 28.1 miles (45.2 kilometers) on Mars, surpassing Opportunity's distance record for any vehicle on a world beyond Earth. NASA's own comparison marks the mileage as of October 11; Perseverance landed in Jezero Crater on February 18, 2021.

Why it matters

This is a distance-and-durability record, not a speed record. A Jet Propulsion Laboratory project manager told Reuters that about 90% of Perseverance's drives are autonomous: the rover uses stereo images, a 3D terrain map and onboard path selection to travel safely between goals despite the communications delay between Earth and Mars.

The Blappos angle

Verified reporting ends above. The Blappos interpretation: the solar system's highest-mileage used car has one owner, 28.1 miles, extensive off-road use and absolutely no local pickup option.
