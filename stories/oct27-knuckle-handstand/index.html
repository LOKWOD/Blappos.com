A Gymnast Holds a Handstand on His Knuckles for 83 Seconds

What happened

Guinness World Records reported October 27 that Tennessee gymnast Madden Shelley earned the male title for longest handstand on the knuckles by balancing for 1 minute 23 seconds. The certified performance happened on September 11, 2025. Shelley said he trained in three phases for more than four months after injuries had interrupted his competitive gymnastics path.

Why it matters

The record is an unusually specific test of balance, body control and pain tolerance, not a general handstand recommendation. Shelley said the position compressed his knuckles beyond perpendicular and caused swelling after attempts. The certification date, exact category and long training timeline keep a striking result from becoming a safe-looking challenge for an untrained viewer.
