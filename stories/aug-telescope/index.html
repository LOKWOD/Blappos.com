NASA launches the universe’s new auditor

What happened

NASA launched the $4 billion Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope from Florida aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy, sending it toward an observing post roughly one million miles from Earth.

Why it matters

Roman combines Hubble-class sharpness with a far wider field of view, enabling huge surveys of galaxies, dark matter, dark energy and exoplanets during its planned five-year mission.
