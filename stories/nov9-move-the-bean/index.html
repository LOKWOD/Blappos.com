Chicago Forms a 250-Person Committee to Move The Bean Seven Inches

What happened

UPI reported on November 9 that hundreds of people gathered at Chicago's Cloud Gate on Sunday after flyers requested a coordinated attempt to move the 110-ton sculpture seven inches to the left at precisely 12:47 p.m. An attendee estimated about 250 people joined the push. The invitation's claim that a man was trapped inside and needed a pickle was part of an established internet joke; organizer Taylor Bennett later clarified that the sculpture did not move at all.

Why it matters

The failed relocation was still a successful public gathering: attendees told local reporting they knew the sculpture would not move and valued the chance to meet people. The story also rewards basic claim hygiene. 'Hundreds' is a reported crowd description, 250 is one attendee's estimate, seven inches was the joke's target, and zero movement was the reported outcome. None of those numbers proves an official attendance count or an engineering attempt authorized by the city.
