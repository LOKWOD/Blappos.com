Tucson's Newest Coyote Appears to Be a 60-Pound Dog

What happened

KJZZ's Fronteras Desk reported on November 18 that Tucson homeowner Patricia Wilson's trail cameras repeatedly recorded a large domestic dog traveling with coyotes over roughly two weeks. Wilson nicknamed the dog Sombra and estimated it at about 60 pounds. Early speculation linked it to Raven, a dog that had escaped from Pima Animal Care Center, but shelter officials said the markings did not match. PACC said intervention is difficult because a trap must capture the dog without trapping coyotes and that it would step in if the dog appeared to be in danger.

Why it matters

The footage documents repeated association; it does not identify the dog, establish its breed, prove ownership or guarantee that the arrangement is safe. Wildlife specialists say coyotes sometimes tolerate dogs, though the behavior is unusual. That combination makes the case useful beyond the novelty: rescue plans must weigh an animal's apparent condition against the risk that an ordinary trap or chase could harm the dog, the coyotes or both.
