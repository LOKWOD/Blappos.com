Brooklyn Gives the Statue of Liberty a 43.5-Second Paint Job

What happened

Guinness World Records reported on November 19 that performance painter David Garibaldi created a recognizable Statue of Liberty painting in 43.5 seconds on the Brooklyn waterfront. The canvas began upside down; after broad green and blue strokes, he flipped it to reveal the image. An on-site Guinness adjudicator confirmed a new title for the fastest time to paint a landmark statue and Garibaldi's first Guinness World Records title.

Why it matters

The memorable number is an adjudicated elapsed time, not an estimate from an edited clip. It applies to one defined record category and one completed painting; it does not establish a universal speed-painting record, say how long preparation took or judge the work's artistic quality. Keeping the category, result and adjudicator together prevents a 43.5-second performance from becoming a broader claim than the evidence supports.
