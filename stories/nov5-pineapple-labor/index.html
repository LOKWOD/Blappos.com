Pineapple Wins the Labor Dispute on a Filing Deadline

What happened

The Guardian reported on November 5 that Brisbane chef Davide Tamburella had been dismissed from the now-closed La Costa restaurant after several workplace concerns, one of which was his repeated refusal to put ham and pineapple on pizzas for a large function. He told management the direction offended his Italian culture and acknowledged it was the third time in four years that he had refused it. The employer also cited language, leadership and escalation concerns; the topping was not the only stated reason for dismissal.

Why it matters

Australia's Fair Work Commission dismissed Tamburella's application because it was filed after the statutory deadline and the commissioner found no exceptional circumstances for an extension. The commission therefore did not rule that pineapple belongs on pizza, decide whether the refusal alone justified dismissal or determine the merits of his broader workplace claims. The news hook is a real employment-law decision whose funniest ingredient was not actually adjudicated.
