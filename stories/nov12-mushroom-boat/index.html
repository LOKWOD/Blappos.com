California Grows a 107-Pound Mushroom and Calls It Maritime

What happened

UPI reported on November 12 that artist Sam Shoemaker earned a Guinness World Records title for the longest solo journey by fungal-mycelium boat. In August 2025, he paddled a 12-foot, 107-pound kayak grown entirely from Ganoderma polychromum mycelium 26.37 miles from Catalina Island to Cabrillo Beach in about 12 hours. Guinness says the vessel took seven weeks to grow after roughly three months of preparation and testing, then received shellac and beeswax before launch.

Why it matters

The achievement is a functional-material demonstration and an endurance record, not proof that fungal boats are generally safer, cheaper or better than conventional manufacturing. Guinness published its feature on November 10 and UPI followed on November 12, while the certified voyage happened in 2025. Shoemaker himself told Guinness that he does not think fungal material is necessarily a better alternative to an injection-molded plastic boat; the value is in testing what a grown object can do and publishing the process.
