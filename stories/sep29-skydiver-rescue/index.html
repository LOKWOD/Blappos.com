Skydivers parachute into a Wisconsin cornfield to aid plane-crash survivors

What happened

Three experienced skydivers aboard a Skydive Milwaukee aircraft saw a single-engine plane carrying four people crash into a cornfield near East Troy on September 27. After the jump pilot reported the crash, the three parachuted near the difficult-to-reach site. At least two had former paramedic training, and one treated a woman with a serious head injury until ground responders arrived. The Guardian reported the rescue on September 29.

Why it matters

All four people aboard the crashed plane survived; three went to a hospital and one was treated at the scene. The result depended on a rare overlap of timing, skill and emergency training, not on spectators improvising a dangerous jump. The National Transportation Safety Board opened an investigation into the crash, so the cause remains an official inquiry rather than a Blappos conclusion.

The Blappos angle

Verified facts end above. The Blappos interpretation: first responders arrived by the only route the cornfield had left open—straight down.
