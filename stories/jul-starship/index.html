Starship aborts before adding another crater

What happened

SpaceX’s Starship triggered a last-second automated abort before its 13th test flight from Texas, postponing launch while engineers investigated the cause.

Why it matters

The stop showed the useful tension between SpaceX’s rapid test cadence and the safety systems designed to catch a bad condition before ignition.

The Blappos angle

The rocket practiced the rare maneuver called restraint.
