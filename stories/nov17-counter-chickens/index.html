Massachusetts Convenience Store Receives Four Live Chickens at Checkout

What happened

Boston 25 reported on November 17 that Hadley police released surveillance images while seeking two people connected with four live chickens dumped onto a convenience-store counter shortly before 2 a.m. Monday. Police said one person carried the birds in a large tote and the other appeared to record the incident. The chickens appeared uninjured and were placed with an animal rescue while investigators tried to identify the people and the birds' owner.

Why it matters

The spectacle is funny; the unresolved animal-welfare and ownership questions are not. Police were still investigating where the chickens came from and said charges related to their treatment and taking could follow. That means the images support a sequence—tote, counter, phone and birds—but not a conviction, a known motive, proof of theft or permission to identify unnamed people as criminals.
