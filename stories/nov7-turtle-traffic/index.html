New Rochelle Assigns the State Reptile to Traffic Duty

What happened

News 12 Westchester reported on November 7 that a New Rochelle police officer moved a large turtle out of a busy roadway on Friday and relocated it to a safer area. The report cites photographs shared by the department. A separate local account identified the animal as a common snapping turtle near Pine Brook Lake; the exact weight, age and destination were not reported.

Why it matters

Road crossings are a serious mortality risk for long-lived turtles. New York's Department of Environmental Conservation advises motorists to avoid sudden swerves, slow near wetlands and, only when it is safe, help a turtle to the shoulder in the direction it was already traveling. DEC specifically urges extreme caution around snapping turtles and warns never to lift one by the tail. This report documents a trained responder's intervention, not a general instruction to imitate the pictured handling.
