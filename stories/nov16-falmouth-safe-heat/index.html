Maine Finds $55,737 in the Woods and Turns It Into Heat

What happened

A November 16 report said Falmouth, Maine, put $55,737 from an abandoned safe toward local heating assistance. A hiker found the cash-filled safe in woods behind the town's Central Fire Station in August 2022. Police traced the container to a New Hampshire bank, confirmed that it had not been reported stolen and followed the required process for trying to identify an owner; no one established a valid claim. The Town Council then directed the unclaimed money to heating aid.

Why it matters

The ending is verified; the origin remains a mystery. Reporting says Maine law required the money to serve a charitable purpose once the ownership process was exhausted, and Falmouth already administers assistance for basic needs including heat. That makes the transfer a documented municipal decision—not evidence that investigators solved where the cash came from, that every applicant will receive a fixed amount or that the fund replaces the town's normal eligibility review.
