THE STORY BEHIND THE SATIRE · Snohomish County, Washington · Sep 9, 2026

Escaped goat becomes acting neighborhood security director

What happened

Snohomish County sheriff’s deputies were called to wrangle an escaped goat with roughly one-foot horns after residents reported it was trying to ram people. Body-camera video captured deputies physically steering the very determined animal back into its pen.

Why it matters

Nobody was reported seriously hurt, but residents stayed inside while deputies handled the animal. The sheriff’s office described the goat as taking its role as neighborhood menace very seriously.

The Blappos angle

For one afternoon the cul-de-sac had a new HOA president. Dues were payable in hay and all complaints were handled forehead-first.
