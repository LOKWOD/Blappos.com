Account gains 10 million followers by finally keeping quiet

What happened

Apple’s main account on X has accumulated nearly 10 million followers while showing zero regular public posts on its timeline. The company has used the platform for paid promotions, while support accounts and executives publish normally.

Why it matters

The blank feed is a strange exception to the standard social-media growth playbook. It also shows the difference between organic timeline posts and paid advertising: an account can appear silent while the company remains active elsewhere on the platform.

The Blappos angle

Ten million people subscribed to hear nothing and have never been disappointed. Every social-media manager just stared at next month’s content calendar and whispered, “Son of a bitch.”
