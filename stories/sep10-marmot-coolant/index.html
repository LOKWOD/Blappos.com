Mystery marmot treats radiator fluid like a tasting menu

What happened

A mystery marmot found in San Francisco reportedly drank radiator fluid and required help from animal-care professionals. The unusual rescue became one of September 10’s standout odd-news stories.

Why it matters

Antifreeze can be dangerously toxic to animals, and wildlife that wanders around vehicles may encounter leaked automotive fluids. The episode is funny as a headline but also a reminder to clean spills and keep chemicals secured.

The Blappos angle

The marmot arrived in San Francisco, skipped the sourdough and went directly to the fluorescent-green house special. The sommelier has been dismissed.
