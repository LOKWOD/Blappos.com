Another British premiership reaches checkout

What happened

Keir Starmer formally resigned to King Charles, clearing the way for Andy Burnham to become Britain’s next prime minister after another leadership crisis.

Why it matters

The handover added to Britain’s recent run of shortened premierships and left a new government facing weak growth, strained public services and an electorate exhausted by resets.
