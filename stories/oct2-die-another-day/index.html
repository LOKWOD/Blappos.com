Die Another Day

What happened

On September 30, Tennessee attempted to execute death-row prisoner Christa Pike at Riverbend Maximum Security Institution in Nashville using pentobarbital. Officials administered the protocol's initial dose and a second dose, but Pike remained alive. Reuters reported on October 2 that her lawyers said she was unconscious, intubated and on a ventilator in critical condition.

Why it matters

Tennessee's Department of Correction said the written protocol permits no further procedure beyond the steps carried out that night. Governor Bill Lee suspended the state's remaining executions for 2026 and ordered a third-party review. The review had not yet established why the drugs failed to kill Pike, so the medical cause remains unresolved.
