THE STORY BEHIND THE SATIRE · Washington, D.C. · Apr 30

Record shutdown finally finds an ending

What happened

Congress passed and President Trump signed a bipartisan bill funding most of the Department of Homeland Security, ending a shutdown that had stretched beyond 70 days.

Why it matters

The impasse disrupted airports and FEMA operations, left workers unpaid and separated immigration-enforcement funding from the rest of the department’s budget.

The Blappos angle

The government reopened after locating the government.
