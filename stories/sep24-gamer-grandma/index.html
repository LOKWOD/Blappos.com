78-year-old Scottish gamer becomes oldest female Fortnite streamer on Twitch

What happened

A Scottish streamer earned a Guinness World Record at age 78 as the oldest female Fortnite streamer on Twitch, according to a September 24 UPI report. The record recognizes a current public streaming activity, not a one-time publicity stunt.

Why it matters

The record pushes back on the idea that online play and creator culture belong only to young people. Streaming combines gameplay, live presentation, audience interaction and technical setup; doing it publicly at 78 turns an individual hobby into a visible example of lifelong participation in digital culture.
