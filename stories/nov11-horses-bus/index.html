Detroit Bus Briefly Runs a Two-Horse Powertrain

What happened

UPI reported on November 11 that the Detroit Department of Transportation was investigating a viral video showing two horses briefly walking through a city bus near 7 Mile and Strasburg. Video-maker Ellessia Drew recorded two men leading the horses aboard and out through the rear door. Urban City Cowboys co-founder Jordan Williams said the group was leaving a Saturday fundraiser, the bus was stopped and the driver gave permission for the video.

Why it matters

This was a brief staged walk-through, not a newly approved animal-transport service or a claim that the horses completed a bus trip. DDOT said the incident was not consistent with its policy and could create an unsafe and uncomfortable ride for passengers. That official response is the operational boundary: a funny clip can still expose a real gap between an employee's one-off permission and the rules of a public transit system.
