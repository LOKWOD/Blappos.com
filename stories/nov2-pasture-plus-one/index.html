A Bull and a Donkey Turn the Pasture Into a Methods Section

What happened

UPI reported on November 2 that researchers studying geladas in Ethiopia instead documented a selective socio-sexual association between Achille, a bull, and Patroclo, a male donkey. The Current Biology paper records 51 mounting events from March 6 to March 19, 2026, alongside repeated licking, rubbing, nuzzling and close proximity. Both animals had access to other potential partners, and another male donkey showed avoidance when the bull approached him.

Why it matters

The scientifically useful finding is the pattern around the mounting behavior: repeated proximity, selective affiliation and a contrast with another donkey’s avoidance. It broadens the record of same-sex and interspecies behavior beyond reproduction. It does not establish a human-style sexual identity, reveal exactly what either animal felt, prove a lifelong bond or explain the behavior’s evolutionary function; the authors present a single observational case and questions for future research.
