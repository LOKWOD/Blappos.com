Minnesota Gives a Spider Monkey a Heated Squad-Car Upgrade

What happened

Hoodline reported on November 15 that Albert Lea police found a brown spider monkey named McNugget loose in cold weather. The department's public account says officers placed the friendly animal in a squad car to warm up and reunited her with her owner. A local report described McNugget as diaper-wearing, said she had been traveling with her truck-driver owner and reported no injuries.

Why it matters

A warm vehicle and a quick reunion were a good immediate outcome, but the available reporting does not establish how McNugget got loose, how long she was outside or whether any legal exception applied to her ownership. The police department's line that she asked for a lawyer and a banana was plainly a joke, not evidence about animal communication. That boundary matters whenever an agency's comic social copy becomes the internet's factual record.
