THE STORY BEHIND THE SATIRE · French Mountains · Sep 21, 2026

Cow leaves remote French mud by helicopter; ground transport unavailable

What happened

A cow named Vahine became trapped in deep mud in a remote mountain wetland in France. Vehicles could not reach her, so rescuers secured her in a broad sling and lifted her out beneath a helicopter. The September 21 report said she recovered safely.

Why it matters

Remote livestock rescue combines animal welfare, aviation, rigging and terrain decisions. A controlled sling lift can avoid the injuries that pulling a heavy animal through deep mud might cause, but it requires trained crews and careful coordination. The unusual footage shows why the safest route can be the one that never touches the road.

The Blappos angle

Vahine selected the only upgrade above premium economy: external cargo. The mud kept her luggage, but ground transport has been cancelled until further notice.
