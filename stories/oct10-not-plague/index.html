WHO says a Russian anti-plague lab death was not plague; the cause remains unknown

What happened

Reuters reported on October 10 that the World Health Organization still did not know what caused the pneumonia that killed a worker at an anti-plague research center in Siberia on October 2. Russia's regulator said tests of the worker and her contacts were negative for the high-threat pathogens tested, about 200 people had been quarantined, contacts remained symptom-free, and no linked severe-pneumonia cases or evidence of ongoing transmission had been identified.

Why it matters

The two-part finding matters: the available evidence did not support plague or ongoing spread, but it also did not establish a diagnosis. Keeping a verified negative separate from an unresolved cause is how public-health communication can reduce unfounded alarm without turning uncertainty into a false all-clear.
