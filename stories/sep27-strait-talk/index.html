Iran says diplomacy is the only route after U.S. rejects Strait of Hormuz plan

What happened

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian said on September 27 that only diplomacy could resolve the conflict after President Donald Trump publicly rejected a proposal intended to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Reuters reported that the plan was transmitted through Qatar during the U.N. General Assembly and that Iran tied reopening the waterway to a broader agreement; mediators had not formally relayed the U.S. rejection to Tehran when the report was filed.

Why it matters

The Strait is a critical route for energy and commercial shipping, so the difference between a public rejection and a formally transmitted negotiating position matters. The statement did not produce an agreement or reopen the waterway. It did show that both the physical shipping channel and the diplomatic channel remained central to what happens next.
