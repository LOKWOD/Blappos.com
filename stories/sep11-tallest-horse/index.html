THE STORY BEHIND THE SATIRE · Towcester, England · Sep 10, 2026

World’s tallest living horse now needs upstairs neighbors

What happened

Hugo, a four-year-old Shire horse in Towcester, England, was recognized in Guinness World Records 2027 as the world’s tallest living horse. A veterinarian measured him without horseshoes at 1.99 meters—about 6 feet 6 inches—at the withers.

Why it matters

Shire horses were bred for heavy work and are among the world’s largest horse breeds. Hugo is still young, gentle and reportedly affectionate, while the breed itself is now critically endangered.

The Blappos angle

Hugo is tall enough to inspect the second floor without entering the building. His owner does not lead him into a stable; she asks the stable for clearance.
