Japanese bicycle mechanic fixes crash damage for free; rider returns with lunch

What happened

An Indian woman living in Japan said she fell from her bicycle in the rain and bent its handlebar. According to the September 27 report, a nearby bicycle-shop owner first checked whether she was hurt, repaired the bicycle during his lunch break, refused payment and gave her wet-road safety advice. She later returned with a hot meal as thanks.
