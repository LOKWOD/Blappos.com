A Loose Cabin Cat Delays a Taxiing Jet and Still Lands on Time

What happened

Boston.com reported on November 22 that a cat escaped its kennel aboard Delta Flight 2706 while the Boston-to-Tampa aircraft was taxiing Friday morning. The crew paused on the taxiway until the cat was safely secured. In circulated air-traffic-control audio, the pilot explained that a cat had been loose in the cabin; a controller replied that it was a new one and signed off with a meow. Delta said the flight then departed normally and arrived in Tampa on time.

Why it matters

A brief ground hold kept an unsecured animal from becoming an in-flight safety problem, and the on-time arrival shows why incident duration and operational outcome both belong in the headline. The report does not identify the cat or explain how the kennel opened. The controller's joke is an authenticated part of the reported exchange, but it is not evidence that air-traffic policy has added a feline dialect.
