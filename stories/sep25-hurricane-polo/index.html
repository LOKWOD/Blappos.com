Hurricane Polo returns to Category 5 as Baja California watches its turn

What happened

Hurricane Polo strengthened again to Category 5 intensity on September 25, with maximum sustained winds reported at 180 mph. At 2 p.m. Mountain time it was about 395 miles south of Cabo San Lucas and moving west at 10 mph. Forecasters expected a turn toward Baja California Sur, where landfall was possible the following week; the forecast also called for dangerous surf, rip currents and four to eight inches of rain, with isolated totals near 10 inches.

Why it matters

A Category 5 rating describes the storm's peak wind intensity over open water, not the exact damage any one place will receive. Track, timing, rainfall and local terrain determine the real risk. Residents and travelers should follow official forecasts and local emergency instructions because flooding, mudslides and surf can become life-threatening before the center arrives.
