Massachusetts Opens the Cannabis Drive-Thru and Checks Everybody Twice

What happened

Boston.com reported on November 23 that Massachusetts cannabis drive-thru sales were beginning to roll out after the Cannabis Control Commission clarified the rules. The Commission's November 17 action allows licensed adult-use retailers and medical establishments to propose drive-thru service under existing security regulations. The official requirements include Commission review of the plan, camera coverage, local approvals, transactions hidden from public view and age verification for every person in the vehicle both at entry and at the point of sale.

Why it matters

The double ID check is not comic bureaucracy invented for a headline; it is the state's answer to a retail format in which several people can enter a controlled sales area at once. The Commission says drive-thrus may improve access for patients and consumers with mobility limitations, but approval is not automatic. Each operator still needs a compliant security plan and any required municipal permission, and products cannot be visible from the window.
