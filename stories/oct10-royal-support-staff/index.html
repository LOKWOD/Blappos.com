Two school support dogs become homecoming prince and princess

What happened

The Associated Press reported on October 10 that Norene and Polenta, two golden Labrador-retriever crosses, now work at Evergreen High School as the first Canine Companions facility dogs permanently matched with a school after a campus shooting. The dogs joined in March and August, receive years of specialized training, and were recently crowned homecoming prince and princess in capes and crowns.

Why it matters

The crowns are charming, but the durable story is ongoing care after trauma. The dogs and their trained handlers provide a stable, voluntary source of comfort in hallways, classrooms and stressful moments; Canine Companions provides the dogs free, and facility assignments can last eight to ten years.
