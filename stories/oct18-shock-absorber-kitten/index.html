First Responders Extract the Shock-Absorber Kitten

What happened

WSB-TV reported October 18 that Sandy Springs firefighters and police officers safely removed a kitten trapped inside a car’s shock absorber. The report says the kitten, now named Friday Shadow, was recovering well and had already found a permanent home. No injury to a responder was reported.

Why it matters

The story is a compact example of why an animal inside vehicle machinery is a responder job, not an invitation for improvised extraction. The published report confirms the component, agencies and happy outcome, but it does not provide a full incident timeline or explain how the kitten entered the assembly; Blappos does not invent those missing mechanics.
