Space industry hires lichens

What happened

Imperial College London researchers engineered fast-growing lichens as a potential biological feedstock for manufacturing materials beyond Earth.

Why it matters

Organisms that grow using light and limited inputs could help astronauts make useful products without paying to launch every kilogram of raw material from Earth.

The Blappos angle
