ABOUT BLAPPOS

An appraisal firm
for bad ideas.

Blappos is a product-discovery magazine for things that are unnecessary, overbuilt, clever, ridiculous, or suspiciously worth buying anyway.

Why “appraisals”?

The domain originally belonged to an appraisal-technology idea. The new Blappos keeps the useful part of that DNA: every object gets examined, scored and given a ruling. We are not trying to be an infinite catalog. We are trying to have an opinion.

What gets in?

An item should create an immediate reaction: “that is ridiculous,” “I did not know that existed,” or “dammit, I kind of want it.” It should also be a real product from a real company with a real destination link.

How Blappos makes money

At launch, it does not need to. The architecture is affiliate-ready: outbound product links can later become tracked affiliate links, clearly disclosed, without changing the editorial copy or scores. Display advertising and sponsorship can wait until there is traffic worth monetizing.

The rule

We do not praise something because it pays better. If a product is dumb, the page should be allowed to say it is dumb. That is the brand.
