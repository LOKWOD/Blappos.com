Blappos turns verified odd news into illustrated satire. This ledger shows the reporting trail, the strongest available primary evidence, and the point where the facts stop and the joke begins.

Before an odd-news headline: seven checks for time, evidence and claim ceiling

  1. Date: Confirm when the event happened, not only when an article was published.
  2. Origin: Find the named institution, record body, paper, agency or firsthand account behind the headline.
  3. Specificity: Record the exact place, measurement, species, instrument or observation instead of repeating a vague viral summary.
  4. Independent wording: Compare the news report with the primary material and summarize both in original language.
  5. Claim ceiling: State what the evidence does not prove. Organic carbon is not proof of life; a pale turkey is not a new species.
  6. Duplicate event: Check the complete site inventory, source URL, location, people and underlying incident.
  7. Separation: Keep fact, inference, satire and commerce visibly distinct on the page.

California Grows a 107-Pound Mushroom and Calls It Maritime

Fact: Guinness World Records documents Sam Shoemaker's 26.37-mile crossing in a 12-foot, 107-pound Ganoderma polychromum vessel. Claim ceiling: the record does not establish mushroom hulls as a general replacement for plastic boats. Satire: “seaworthiness: cultured” is Blappos commentary. Commerce: the magnet and book links are optional products, not evidence.

Primary record: Guinness World Records · Dated report: UPI · Blappos story and magnet

Mars Hides Organic Carbon Under One Sheet of Dust

Fact: the open-access Science Advances paper reports organic carbon associated with dust-coated Martian rock surfaces observed by Perseverance. Claim ceiling: organic molecules can arise through biological or non-biological processes and are not proof of past life. Satire: “organic does not mean alive” is a deliberately blunt Blappos caption. Commerce: the magnet and reading list are separate from the scientific evidence.

Primary paper: Science Advances via PubMed Central · Dated digest: ScienceDaily · Blappos story and magnet

Wisconsin Turkey Enters Its Smoke Phase

Fact: Wisconsin DNR's Snapshot Wisconsin program explains that a trail-camera turkey with diluted gray plumage is consistent with a rare color condition. Claim ceiling: a photograph cannot establish a new species or supernatural cause. Satire: the “smoke phase” line is Blappos commentary. Commerce: the magnet and bird books do not support the zoological claim.

Primary explanation: Wisconsin DNR · Dated report: UPI · Blappos story and magnet

How the labels work

Fact is directly supported by the linked reporting or primary reference. Inference is a cautious explanation that goes beyond the raw observation and is labeled as such. Satire is the Blappos punchline and artwork. Commerce includes Printify magnets and Amazon affiliate links; it is never presented as sourcing.
