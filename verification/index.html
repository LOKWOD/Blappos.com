Ridiculous news, checked line by line

This public ledger shows how the latest Blappos stories were verified. Each entry names the dated source, identifies any stronger primary evidence, records what the evidence does—and does not—establish, and marks where the joke begins. It is designed to make a correction easier, not to make an absurd story look more certain than its sources allow.

EXTREME-LIFE SCIENCE · LASSEN VOLCANIC NATIONAL PARK

A “fire amoeba” raises the known heat limit for eukaryotes

Verified claim: The newly described Incendiamoeba cascadensis divided by mitosis at 63°C (145.4°F), remained active at 64°C (147.2°F) and recovered after a brief 70°C (158°F) exposure. It was collected from a neutral-pH hot-spring tributary near Drakesbad in Lassen Volcanic National Park.

Evidence: Reuters’ September 22 science report identifies the organism, field location, experimental thresholds and Cell publication, attributing the work to Syracuse University biologists Beryl Rappaport and Angela Oliverio.

Boundary: The temperatures and cellular behavior are reported research findings. “Complex life runs hot” is satire, and the illustrated amoeba is a mascot rather than a microscopy image or anatomical reconstruction.

REUSABLE REFERENCE

The five-step strange-news verification checklist

  1. Date the claim: record when the event happened and when the reporting was published; a resurfaced clip is not a new event.
  2. Find the closest source: prefer the responsible agency, record authority, institution, named witness or original research over an unattributed repost.
  3. Preserve attribution: distinguish what a source proves from what a witness, company or expert says. Do not silently upgrade “appears to be” into “is.”
  4. Check the consequence: verify the number, location, safety implication and outcome that make the story matter—not merely its most shareable detail.
  5. Write the boundary: state the unresolved point, identify the illustration as artwork and place satire after the verified facts.

Wildlife evidence ladder: agency confirmation outranks a qualified identification, which outranks a witness description, which outranks an unattributed social post. When the strongest available evidence sits below formal confirmation, the headline and copy must say so.
