## Proposed story

**Title:** HYROX changes hygiene rules after Beijing competitor was allowed to continue racing

**What happened:** At a September 12 HYROX event in Beijing, Australian athlete Joanna Wietrzyk experienced bowel incontinence and continued competing. She later apologized, said she should have left the course, and withdrew her result. HYROX co-founder Moritz Fürste apologized for the organizers' response and said event procedures and rules were being changed.

**Why it matters:** Competitors share indoor lanes and workout equipment. A race needs a clear way for officials to stop competition and clean a contamination hazard promptly, while treating the affected athlete with dignity.

**The Blappos angle:** The race had eight workout stations and, apparently, no official empowered to call a time-out for the ninth. The runner did not write the rulebook; the organization has now discovered why the rulebook needed a mop chapter.
